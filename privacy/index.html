Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Future You is an iPhone app for daily visualization practice. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: there is no account, no ads and no tracking, and your goals stay on your phone unless you use a feature that needs a server.

What stays on your device

Your name, goals, scenes, check-ins, evidence log, streak and settings are stored on your iPhone. We do not run a database of users and we cannot see this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

  • Coach and reality previews. When you message the coach or generate a reality preview, the text you send, together with the goal it relates to, is passed through our server (a Cloudflare Worker) to OpenAI to create a reply. Our server does not store or log the content of these messages. To prevent abuse, it keeps short-lived usage counters tied to a random device identifier and your IP address, which expire within two days. OpenAI handles them under its API data usage policy.
  • Voice narration. Guided scenes are read aloud with a voice built into iOS. If a third-party voice service is turned on, the text of the scene is sent to that service to create the audio, which is then cached on your device.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Notifications

Reminders and daily messages are scheduled on your device with iOS local notifications. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children

Future You is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
