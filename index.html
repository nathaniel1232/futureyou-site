Future You is a daily 21-day visualization practice. Picture your goal as already done, feel what that is like, then go through your day as the person who got there.

Coming soon to the App Store

A few minutes a day, three steps.

  1. 01

    See it

    A guided scene puts you in the moment your goal is already done. Where you are, who is there, what you notice.

  2. 02

    Feel it

    Stay in the scene long enough to feel it as real. The feeling is what your mind holds on to.

  3. 03

    Be it

    Pick one thing future you would do today, and do it. Small actions turn the picture into habit.

Built on the psychology of self-image.

People tend to act in line with the picture they hold of themselves, and a new self-image takes repeated practice to settle in.

Future You turns that idea into a 21-day routine: one clear picture of who you are becoming, rehearsed every day.

What is in the app.

  • Daily guided scene

    A short visualization for your goal, read aloud so you can close your eyes.

  • Coach

    Talk through what is in the way and get a next step you can take today.

  • Reminders

    A nudge at the time you choose, so the practice happens every day.

  • Progress

    Your day out of 21, your streak, and how real the picture feels as the weeks go on.
